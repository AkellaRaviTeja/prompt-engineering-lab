- Walking time, administration building to Gate 2 staff car park: 5 minutes.
- Walking time, administration building to stairwell C (top): 3 minutes.
- Walking time, stairwell C (bottom) to Gate 2: 4 minutes.
- Walking time, supervisor's hut to stairwell C: 2 minutes.
- Stairwell C has two doors. STAIR-C-TOP opens onto the admin walkway. STAIR-C-BOTTOM opens onto
