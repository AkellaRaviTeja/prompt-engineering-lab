# Letter from defence counsel

**From:** Marcus Hale, Hale Brennan LLP, counsel to Halcyon Maritime Logistics
**To:** Inspector A. Varma, Economic Offences Wing
**Date:** 11 April

Inspector,

I understand the Economic Offences Wing is using a language model to analyse material seized from
my client. I put you on notice of three concerns.

First, these systems are known to give different answers to the same question. If your machine
produced one reading of a document on Tuesday and another on Wednesday, then neither reading is
evidence. It is a coin toss.

Second, I will ask for the exact instructions given to the machine, the exact model and the exact
settings used for every finding you rely on. If you cannot reproduce a finding on demand, in court,
I will ask the judge to exclude it.

Third, I expect any summary produced by the machine to be checked against the source by a person.

I trust the EOW will not object to these ordinary standards.

Yours faithfully,
Marcus Hale
