# Witness statement: Raj Malhotra

**Role:** Head of Operations, Halcyon Maritime Logistics
**Taken by:** DS P. Iyer, 16 March, 14:00
**Status:** Signed

On 14 March I was in the Pier 9 administration building until late, preparing the berthing plan for
the two vessels held outside the breakwater. Tomas Reyes was also in the building. We spoke briefly
in the corridor at around 22:45. He said he was going to check something on the pier. He seemed
tired but not upset.

I finished work, left the administration building at about 23:30 and walked straight to my car in
the Gate 2 staff car park. I drove home and arrived at about 00:05. I did not go near stairwell C.
I did not see Tomas again.

Ivan Petrov called me at 23:58 and told me what had happened. I drove back to the pier and was
there when the ambulance arrived. I called Elena Voss at 00:40.

I have nothing to add about Northwind Freight. It is one of many freight forwarders we use. Vendor
payments are a finance matter, not operations.

R. Malhotra
