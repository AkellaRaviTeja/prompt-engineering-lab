# Witness statement: Ivan Petrov

**Role:** Night dock supervisor, Pier 9, Halcyon Maritime Logistics
**Taken by:** DC L. Mensah, 15 March, 02:10
**Status:** Signed

I came on shift at 18:00 on 14 March. The night was quiet. Two vessels were due but both were held
outside the breakwater for weather, so there was no loading after 21:00.

I did my rounds of the north yard and the reefer stacks between 23:00 and 23:30. Everything was
normal. I went back to the supervisor's hut by the Gate 2 road and made tea. The hut is about
ninety metres from stairwell C, maybe two minutes on foot.

At about twenty to twelve I heard a bang. I thought a container door had swung open in the wind,
which happens. I finished my tea and then went to check. I went past the reefer stacks first
because that is where the doors usually come loose. Then I went round to stairwell C.

I found Mr Reyes at the bottom of the stairs at 23:52. I know the time because I looked at my radio
when I called it in. He was not moving. I did not touch him apart from checking for a pulse. I did
not see anyone else on the pier. I did not see any car leave.

I have known Mr Reyes for six years. He sometimes stayed late. He was always polite to the night
crew. I did not know he was on the pier that night.

I. Petrov
