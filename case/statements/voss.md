# Witness statement: Elena Voss

**Role:** Chief Executive Officer, Halcyon Maritime Logistics
**Taken by:** DS P. Iyer, 16 March, 10:30, in the presence of counsel (M. Hale)
**Status:** Signed

Tomas Reyes was our CFO for nine years. He was meticulous and respected, and his death is a great
loss to the company and to me personally.

On the evening of 14 March I was at the Kestrel Bay Chamber of Commerce dinner at the Harbour Hotel
from 19:30 until after midnight. Several hundred people saw me there. I learned of Tomas's death
from Raj Malhotra by telephone at 00:40.

Tomas had been under strain. The annual audit closed on 28 February and he had worked long hours.
He told me in early March that he wanted to review "some vendor arrangements" before the board
meeting in April. I told him to take the time he needed. I did not ask which vendors. That was his
area and I trusted him with it.

I am not aware of any dispute between Tomas and any member of staff. Halcyon will cooperate fully
with any inquiry.

E. Voss
