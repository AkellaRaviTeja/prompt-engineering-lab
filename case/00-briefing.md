# EOW briefing note: Halcyon Maritime / death of T. Reyes

**From:** Inspector A. Varma, Economic Offences Wing
**To:** Analyst (seconded)
**Date:** 2 April
**Classification:** Restricted. Evidence remains on LANTERN.

On the night of 14 March, Tomas Reyes (52), Chief Financial Officer of Halcyon Maritime
Logistics, was found at the foot of stairwell C, Pier 9, Kestrel Bay container terminal. The night
dock supervisor, Ivan Petrov, reported finding him at 23:52. The coroner recorded a fall. The case
was closed as accidental on 21 March.

On 29 March an anonymous envelope reached this office. It held a single photocopied page from
Halcyon's payments ledger with one vendor circled: **Northwind Freight Ltd**. Written in the margin,
in what Reyes's family identify as his handwriting: "Not a vendor."

The EOW has reopened the matter as a financial inquiry. We have seized:

- Witness statements (Petrov, Voss, Malhotra)
- Reyes's personal notes, recovered from his office laptop
- Halcyon's payments ledger for January to March
- Supplier invoices from Reyes's mailbox
- Messages from Reyes's work phone, 1 to 14 March
- Pier 9 badge logs and gate camera logs for 14 March

Questions in order: Was the death an accident? Who or what is Northwind Freight? Who benefits?

Everything you find must stand up in court. Assume Halcyon's counsel, Marcus Hale, will read it.

A. Varma
